ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Carl.

I’m a photographer and visual storyteller based in Benguet, documenting moments that feel honest, quiet, and human. My work is rooted in documentary photography — capturing stories as they unfold.

I’ve worked with campus publications, creative organizations, and local productions, covering events, portraits, and behind-the-scenes moments. I’m drawn to natural light, real emotions, and images that feel lived-in.

When I’m not shooting, I’m probably developing film, editing photos, or drinking coffee while thinking about the next story to tell.
